Pizza Recipe

Ingredients

  • Pizza base
  • Pizza sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Onion
  • Capsicum
  • Olives
  • Oregano & chili flakes

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Spread pizza sauce on the base.
  3. Add vegetables evenly.
  4. Top with mozzarella cheese.
  5. Bake for 15–20 minutes.
  6. Sprinkle oregano and chili flakes.
